Frequently asked questions

How much will my diagnostic cost?

Most code reads and live-data checks run R150–R450. Bi-directional actuator tests, which command a part directly instead of just reading a fault code, run R450–R1,200 depending on complexity.

What's the callout fee, and when is it waived?

R100–R150 covers a technician's base service radius (typically 10–15km); beyond that a per-km rate applies. It's waived automatically on any job over R600, and halved for a second vehicle at the same address.

Is the price fixed before the technician arrives?

Yes — you'll see the price band for your job type before confirming. If the technician finds the issue needs a different service once on site, they'll quote that separately before doing any extra work.

What if the fault isn't found or fixed?

Diagnostic fees cover the technician's time and the proof they provide — confirming what's wrong (or ruling faults out). That diagnostic result is the deliverable, separate from any repair work.

How are technicians vetted?

Technicians are verified before they can accept jobs on the network, and every completed job builds a visible rating and history.

How do I pay?

Payment happens through the platform once the job's complete — no cash negotiation on site.
